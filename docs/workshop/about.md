# Workshop

## Aims

The goal of this this hands-on workshop is to refresh your knowledge about Linked Data, to allow you to practice writing RDF statements to describe real-world physical documents, and to let you experience the power of Linked Open Data first hand, by sharing your RDF-descriptions.

## Overview

For this workshop, you will be divided up into groups. Each group will represent a different memory institution; in this case: a borrowing library for vinyl records. Each group will get a different record, and will be asked to describe the record in RDF – to include it into their institution's collection. Afterwards, each instution will share their RDF-statements with the group, which will allow us to visualize the connections between the items in their collections. To achieve this goal, we will take the following steps:

1. Describing your individual document in RDF statements
2. Visualize your relations as graph
3. Merge the RDF statements of all the institutions
4. Visualize the relations between documents (and institutions) as a graph
